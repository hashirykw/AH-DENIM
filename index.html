About AH Denim

Legacy ofexcellence

Since 1999, AH Denim has grown into a leading cut-to-pack manufacturer of denim and woven garments for men, women and kids. Two manufacturing units and in-house laundry let us handle everything from design development to final packaging under one roof.

Cut-to-pack solutions

Every production stage in-house, for tighter quality control and faster turnaround.

Advanced facilities
